# Project Left Hand

A cardiac risk screening tool for dialysis patients.

## Why
My grandfather survived six years of dialysis. We monitored his kidneys obsessively. 
Then his heart killed him in 24 hours because no one connected the pain in his left hand 
to the blockages in his arteries. This tool is the first step toward fixing that gap.

## What it does
- Screens dialysis patients for cardiac risk based on BP, symptoms, and treatment status
- Flags hypertensive crisis (&gt;180) and hypotension (&lt;90) as emergencies
- Warns about vascular access complications in dialysis patients with arm pain
- Reminds that dialysis patients must not take NSAIDs without nephrologist approval
